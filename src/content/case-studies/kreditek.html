The client

Who Kreditek Cooperative is

Kreditek Multipurpose Cooperative Society Ltd trades as Kredicoop. It offers what many Nigerians cannot get from a bank: a place to save with steady returns and borrow without high interest or heavy paperwork. Its terms are simple:

  • A one-time ₦5,000 membership fee.
  • 1% a month (12% a year) earned on savings.
  • After 6 months of saving, a loan of up to 2× total savings, at 4% interest.
  • KrediCare, a plan for saving gradually towards household items, business tools or personal goals.

A cooperative lives on trust. Members hand over money every month, so they need to see that it was received, what it has earned, and when they can borrow.

Where things stood

The problem with running a cooperative by hand

A cooperative's promise only holds if every contribution is collected, recorded and credited correctly, month after month, for every member. Done by hand, that work grows with every new member.

ProblemWhat it cost the cooperative
Collecting money by hand. Members paid by bank transfer or cash, whenever they remembered.Staff spent hours chasing late payments and matching transfers to names. Missed months meant less money saved and less money to lend.
Records in spreadsheets and notebooks. Balances lived in files only staff could see.Errors crept in, members had to ask to know their balance, and disagreements were hard to settle.
Loan decisions worked out manually. Each request meant checking history, adding up savings and calculating the limit.Loans were slow and could be inconsistent from one member to the next.
Joining took paperwork. Identity and bank details were checked by hand.Signing up new members was slow, and mistakes in bank details caused failed payouts.
No way to sign up online. Growth depended on word of mouth and in-person visits.The cooperative could not reach people beyond its existing circle.

What we did

Six changes, each tied to a business result

We use AI to write code faster, but the code is the easy part. The value is in understanding how the cooperative actually runs, turning its rules into software, and making sure money is never lost or double-counted. Each change below starts from a business problem.

A website that explains the cooperative and signs people up

Problem
New members could only join through word of mouth, and people needed the terms explained in person.
What we built
A public website at www.kredicoop.ng that explains the cooperative in plain words: who it is, how membership works step by step, the rates, the terms and conditions, and answers to common questions. Every page is set up to be found on Google, and it works on any phone.
Result
The cooperative can now recruit members around the clock, anywhere in Nigeria. Prospects arrive already knowing the terms, so staff spend less time explaining the basics.

Sign-up with identity checks built in

Problem
Checking who a new member is, and that their bank details are right, was manual. A wrong account number meant a failed or misdirected payout.
What we built
An online sign-up that confirms the member's email and phone number with one-time codes, collects their BVN (the Bank Verification Number every Nigerian bank customer has), and checks their bank account with the bank before saving it, so the account name is confirmed and not just typed. Members cannot reach money features until these steps are complete.
Result
Members can join in minutes from their phone. The cooperative knows who it is dealing with, and payouts go to accounts that have been checked.

Monthly savings that collect themselves

Problem
Contributions depended on members remembering to pay and staff remembering to chase.
What we built
Members choose a monthly amount and how long they want to save. Every morning the system finds the savings that are due that day and collects them automatically, from the member's saved card or through a direct debit from their bank account. Members who prefer transfers get their own personal account number, so every transfer is credited to the right person. The first savings plan starts straight away. Extra plans can go to the cooperative for approval, or be approved automatically when they fall within limits the cooperative sets.
Result
Contributions arrive on time without anyone chasing them, and savings keep growing even when members are busy. That means more money in the cooperative to lend.

Loans decided in seconds, not days

Problem
Every loan request meant staff checking the member's history, adding up their savings and working out how much they could borrow.
What we built
The platform checks eligibility the moment a member opens the loan page: identity verified, membership paid, savings active, at least six months of saving history, and no loan already pending or running. It then shows the most they can borrow, twice their savings. Before applying, the member sees a preview with the interest, fees, total to repay and due date. Repayments are made from the dashboard, and every disbursement is recorded.
Result
Members get an answer immediately, every member is judged by the same rules, and staff no longer do loan sums by hand. Clear terms up front also mean fewer surprises at repayment time.

A dashboard where members see everything

Problem
Members had to ask staff for their balance, and could not see what they had paid or earned.
What we built
Each member has a private dashboard showing their savings plans, loans and repayment dates, a full payment history, notifications, and their saved cards and bank details, which they can add, check or remove themselves.
Result
Members can check their money at any time without calling the office, which builds the trust a cooperative depends on and cuts down routine questions.

One record of every naira, and rules the cooperative controls

Problem
Money records were spread across bank statements, spreadsheets and notebooks.
What we built
A single ledger that records every movement of money (membership fees, monthly savings, loan repayments) with who paid, how, and what it was for. The cooperative's rules, such as the membership fee, the minimum and maximum monthly saving, approval limits and the collection day, are settings the cooperative can change without asking a developer to rewrite anything.
Result
Any payment can be traced in seconds, which makes reconciliation and audits simple. When the cooperative changes its terms, the platform changes with it the same day.
